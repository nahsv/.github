NAHSV Healthlinko Private Limited (CIN: U86909DL2025PTC447318) is a private, non-government company registered with the Registrar of Companies, Delhi. Incorporated on 28 April 2025, NAHSV Healthlinko builds trusted digital health and wellness platforms that make quality care more discoverable, transparent and accessible. Through its brands [MediFyr](https://medifyr.com), [Unikorn](https://unikorn.cloud), [Pregzify](https://pregzify.com) and [Zerunio](https://zerunio.com) the company supports patients and families across online consultations, international treatment journeys, everyday wellness and pregnancy support.
